About Our School

School Of Science was founded with the sole purpose of bringing quality education to the doorstep of every rural family. Located in the heart of our community, we serve students from Classes LKG to 12, offering comprehensive schooling with a strong foundation in science, mathematics, and arts.

Under the visionary leadership of Shri Ramakant Pandey (M.Sc. Mathematics), our school has built a strong reputation over the last two decades. We are equipped with modern laboratories, a digital library, 24x7 CCTV security, school van transport, and a highly dedicated faculty team committed to nurturing the academic and personal growth of every student.

📜

Our History

2005
Started coaching classes for Class 9 and Class 10 students.
2014
Established School Of Science, starting classes from Class 6 to Class 10.
2020
Expanded the school structure to senior secondary levels, serving Classes 6 to 12.
2023
Fully expanded the school from LKG to Class 12, providing comprehensive education for all age groups.
